# Free legacy character library

Downloaded from Kay Lousberg's official itch.io pages on 2026-10-14:

- [Spooktober 1.1](https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-spooktober): Jack and Witch.
- [Mini-Game Variety 1.2](https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kay-kit-mini-game-variety-pack): Bear, Dog, Duck.
- [Dungeon 1.0](https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-dungeon): four adventurers and alternate heads.
- [Skeletons 1.0](https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-skeleton-pack): four skeleton types and broken variants.

Original source ZIPs remain in `C:/Users/k/Downloads`. This project includes the
character glTF files and their referenced buffers/images only, with each pack's
CC0 license. `intake.json` records download hashes and included file counts.
Models are unmodified creator assets. Their JSON has no skins or animations;
they are stored for props/NPCs and future rigging, rather than offered as broken
playable avatars. The ten current rigged playable choices come from Adventurers
2.0 and Skeletons 1.1, including the unhooded/hooded Rogue variants.

Mystery Monthly packs and EXTRA/SOURCE tiers require purchase and are excluded.
No AI-generated model or texture was produced for this intake.
